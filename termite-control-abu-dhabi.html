Termite Control in Abu Dhabi

Silent destroyers need a systematic answer — inspection, soil treatment and timber protection.

Termites cause more structural damage worldwide than any other pest, and they do it quietly. In Abu Dhabi, subterranean termites travel up from the soil into villas and low-rise buildings, hollowing out door frames, skirting boards, pergolas and furniture from the inside. By the time damage is visible, the colony has usually been active for months. American Bite Pest Control inspects, treats and helps protect properties from termite damage.

Signs of termites

Watch for mud tubes climbing foundation walls, wood that sounds hollow when tapped, blistered or bubbling paint on wooden surfaces, and discarded wings near windows after swarming. In gardens, check wooden fences, pergolas and tree stumps. Termites avoid light, so damage hides behind paint and plaster until it's advanced.

How professional termite treatment works

Subterranean termites live in the soil, so treatment has to reach them there. The standard professional approach is a treated zone in the soil around the building's foundation, plus direct treatment of infested timber. Baiting systems are another option for ongoing protection — stations in the ground that the colony feeds on. Villas, compounds and commercial buildings

Villas with gardens and irrigation are at higher risk because moist soil suits termites. Older compounds with wooden fixtures need regular inspection. Commercial buildings should include termite checks in their maintenance schedule — a colony in a document store or wooden-clad lobby is an expensive surprise. New builds can be protected with pre-construction soil treatment, which is far cheaper than curing an infestation later.

Prevention after treatment

Keep soil and mulch away from direct contact with wooden structures, fix leaking taps and irrigation near foundations, and don't store firewood or timber against walls. Annual inspections catch new activity early, when treatment is simple.
